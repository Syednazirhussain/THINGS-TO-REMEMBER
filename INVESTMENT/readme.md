3.  Professional Managment
4.  Affrodability

## Role of Assest Managment Company

1.  Invest according to the investment goal
2.  Research and analysis
3.  Portfolio construction
4.  Performance review

## What is NAV and AUM

-   Number of shares: 5
-   Assest under management (AUM): Rs 5000
-   Net Assest Value (NAV): 5000/5 = Rs 1000
-   Number of share per person: 1

If a person comes with 500 rupees then he will get 0.5 share
And If a person comes with 100 rupees then he will get 0.1 share
