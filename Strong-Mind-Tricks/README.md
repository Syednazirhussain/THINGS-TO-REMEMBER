### 6 Brain Exercises to Strengthen Your Mind
1. Brush your teeth with the opposit hand.
2. Switch your senses.
    - Type without seeing the keyboard.
    - Draw drawing without seeing it.
    - Identify the walking patterns of each individual in your family.
3. Puzzles
    - Playing games like sudoko, solid tire, crossword puzzles, 2048, and visual puzzles.
4. Switching Games
    - OVK
        - Make the sign OK with your left hand and the sign of victory with your right hand. then continue switch signs with your hands 10 times.
    - The Circle Triangle Game
        - Make a circle in the air with your left hand and a triangle with your right hand. Try to make these shapes perfect and continue switch signs with your hands 10 times.
    - Try to write with your both hands.
5. Grew up with the technology
    - Not to use calculator for normal calculations and mobile to write shopping list.
